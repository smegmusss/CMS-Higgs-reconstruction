# $H \to ZZ^* \to 4\mu$ Invariant Mass Output Data

This dataset contains the reconstructed four-muon invariant mass $m_{4\mu}$ values computed from CMS 2012 Open Data using relativistic 4-momentum summation ($m_{4\mu} = \sqrt{E_{\text{tot}}^2 - |\vec{p}_{\text{tot}}|^2}$).

---

## Energy Region Guide ($m_{4\mu}$ Spectrum)

* **$< 100 \text{ GeV}$ (Sub-Threshold Background):** Non-resonant background events, including misidentified leptons from $Z+\text{jets}$, $t\bar{t}$ processes, or low-mass meson decays.
* **$\sim 125 \text{ GeV}$ (Higgs Boson Peak):** The resonant signal peak corresponding to the Standard Model Higgs Boson ($H \to ZZ^* \to 4\mu$). Characterized by one on-shell $Z$ and one off-shell $Z^*$ boson decaying into two muon pairs ($2\mu^+ 2\mu^-$).
* **$130 - 200 \text{ GeV}$ (Continuum $ZZ$ Background):** Non-resonant, irreducible background from direct continuum $q\bar{q} \to ZZ$ pair production.
* **$> 200 \text{ GeV}$ (e.g., $\sim 215 \text{ GeV}$, On-Shell $ZZ$ High Mass):** Events originating from high-energy $ZZ$ pairs produced on-shell with high transverse momentum ($p_T$).
